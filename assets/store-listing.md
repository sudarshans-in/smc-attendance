### Full Description
*(max 4000 characters — current: ~950 characters)*
```
SMC Karmachari is the official attendance and work-tracking app for Silchar Municipal Corporation (SMC) field workers and Safai Karmacharis.

FEATURES FOR FIELD WORKERS
• Mark daily attendance with GPS location verification
• Upload work-progress photos with automatic geo-tagging
• View personal attendance history and work photo log
• Secure login via mobile number OTP

FEATURES FOR SUPERVISORS
• View real-time squad attendance status
• Browse today's work photos uploaded by squad members
• Tap any photo for full-screen view with uploader name and timestamp

SECURITY
• All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encrypted)
• Session tokens stored in Android Keystore (hardware-encrypted)
• Biometric app lock — automatically locks after 30 seconds in background
• No data stored in unencrypted local storage

ACCESS
This app is for registered SMC employees only. Accounts are created by supervisors through the DC Office web portal. Download the app and contact your supervisor to receive your registered mobile number.

Developed for the Cachar District Office, Silchar, Assam.
```

## Notes for Reviewer (App Access section)

If the reviewer asks why certain features require permissions:

- **Precise Location** — required for GPS attendance verification; approximate location is not sufficient to confirm a worker is at their assigned ward/zone
- **Camera** — mandatory selfie photo taken at attendance mark-in; workers also upload work-progress photos during their shift
- **Internet** — all attendance and photo data syncs to the SMC server in real time
